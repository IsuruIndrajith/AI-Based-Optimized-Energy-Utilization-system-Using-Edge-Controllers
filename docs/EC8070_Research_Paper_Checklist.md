# EC8070-RESEARCH Project III: Research Paper Submission Checklist

This guide outlines the key details and components you must include in your research paper to achieve maximum marks based on the official evaluation criteria.

## General Submission Instructions
- **Deadline:** Must be submitted on or before the deadline (late submissions receive zero marks).
- **Submission:** Only **one member** from each group should submit the manuscript.
- **Length Constraint:** Maximum of **four pages**.
- **Formatting:** Pages must be justified. Include a content page with appropriate settings. Ensure grammar is correct and there are no typos.

---

## Section-by-Section Breakdown for Maximum Marks

### 1. Title (05 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (05-04)** score, ensure your title and heading block include:
- [ ] **Clarity & Conciseness:** The title is easy to understand and straight to the point.
- [ ] **Relevance & Specificity:** Clearly reflects the core of the research.
- [ ] **Author Details:** Correctly lists all author details and their order.

### 2. Abstract (10 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (10-07)** score, your abstract must cover:
- [ ] **Purpose and Objectives:** Briefly state why the research was conducted.
- [ ] **Methodology:** Summarize how the research was conducted.
- [ ] **Key Findings:** Highlight the most important results.
- [ ] **Conclusion and Implication:** State the main takeaway and its broader impact.
- [ ] **Structure and Clarity:** Ensure it flows logically and is easy to read.

### 3. Keywords (05 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (05-04)** score, provide keywords that feature:
- [ ] **Relevance:** Directly related to the paper's main topics.
- [ ] **Specificity and Conciseness:** Precise terms, avoiding overly broad words.
- [ ] **Diversity & Number:** Adequate amount of varied keywords to cover the scope.
- [ ] **Searchability:** Terms that other researchers would likely use to find your paper.

### 4. Introduction (10 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (10-07)** score, include the following:
- [ ] **Background Information:** Contextualize the study area.
- [ ] **Research Problem:** Clearly define the issue your research addresses.
- [ ] **Aim and Objectives:** State exactly what the research intends to accomplish.
- [ ] **Literature Review:** Incorporate existing research and establish the gap.
- [ ] **Expected Contributions:** Detail the novel aspects and value your research adds.

### 5. Methodology (20 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (20-13)** score, ensure your methods section is comprehensive:
- [ ] **Clarity and Detail:** Clear step-by-step description of what was done.
- [ ] **Appropriateness:** Justify the chosen methods/architecture with explanations.
- [ ] **Data Collection and Analysis:** Explain how data was gathered and processed.
- [ ] **Ethical Consideration:** Address any ethical issues (if applicable).
- [ ] **Limitations:** Acknowledge constraints within the methodology.
- [ ] **Reproducibility:** Provide enough detail so others can replicate the study.

### 6. Results (20 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (20-13)** score, focus on:
- [ ] **Presentation and Interpretation:** Clearly show results and explain what they mean.
- [ ] **Relevance:** Ensure results tie directly back to the research question.
- [ ] **Data Integrity:** Demonstrate reliable and valid data.
- [ ] **Goal Alignment:** Show whether the findings achieve the stated aim.
- [ ] **Comprehensiveness and Flow:** Logical progression of data presentation.
- [ ] **Supplementary Data:** Include if necessary for clarity (within page limits).
- [ ] **Consistency:** Results must logically follow the stated methodology.

### 7. Discussion (10 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (10-07)** score, the discussion must include:
- [ ] **Interpretation of Results:** Dive deeper into the 'why' behind your findings.
- [ ] **Contextualization:** Compare findings with the literature review.
- [ ] **Implications:** Discuss the real-world or theoretical impact of the results.
- [ ] **Limitations and Future Directions:** What couldn't be done, and what should be done next.
- [ ] **Conclusions:** Wrap up the discussion points logically.

### 8. Conclusion (10 Marks)
To achieve an **Excellent (10-07)** score, your conclusion section should have:
- [ ] **Summary of Key Findings:** Brief recap of the most critical discoveries.
- [ ] **Relevance:** Reiterate how findings answer the main research question.
- [ ] **Limitations Acknowledged:** Briefly restate the study's boundaries.
- [ ] **Recommendations:** Provide actionable recommendations if applicable.

### 9. Clarity and Organization (05 Marks)
To achieve a **Good (05-03)** score (highest bracket for this section):
- [ ] **Formatting:** Pages are justified.
- [ ] **Components:** All necessary sections (Introduction, Literature Review, etc.) are present.
- [ ] **Content Page:** Ensure the content page and its settings are accurate.
- [ ] **Language:** Checked thoroughly for grammar and typos.

### 10. Reference Style (05 Marks)
To achieve a **Good (05-03)** score (highest bracket for this section), ensure:
- [ ] **Completeness and Formatting:** Strictly follow the required citation format.
- [ ] **Accuracy:** Double-check publication years and author names.
- [ ] **Relevance:** Sources should be highly relevant to the paper.
- [ ] **In-Text Citation:** Ensure every reference is cited within the text, and vice versa.
- [ ] **Diversity:** Use a varied selection of high-quality sources.
